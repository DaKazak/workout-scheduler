How is this estimated?

kcal = MET × body weight (kg) × hours. MET values are approximate, from the Compendium of Physical Activities.

  • Running — light 8.3 (7:30 /km), moderate 9.8 (6:00 /km), vigorous 11.8 (5:00 /km)
  • Walking — light 3.0 (15:00 /km), moderate 3.5 (12:00 /km), vigorous 5.0 (~9:20 /km)
  • Cycling — light 6.8 (17 km/h), moderate 8.0 (20 km/h), vigorous 10.0 (24 km/h)
  • Workout (strength / gym) — light 3.5, moderate 5.0, vigorous 6.0; time only

Distance entries are converted to time using the pace shown. Real burn varies with fitness, terrain, rest periods and more — treat it as a ballpark.
